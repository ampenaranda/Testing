For Faculty: Understanding Your Adult Students

Faculty: Your students are adults, not children. They have jobs, families, responsibilities, and rich life experiences. Whether they're in a traditional classroom or learning online asynchronously, they deserve to be treated with the respect and autonomy that adults need.

These scenarios reveal the six principles of adult learning (Andragogy) that help faculty understand their diverse student population. Adult learners have different needs, motivations, and circumstances—especially in online asynchronous settings where faculty sometimes unintentionally treat them like dependent children rather than capable adults. Explore each principle to better serve all your students.
